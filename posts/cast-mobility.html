CAST: A Context-Aware Spatio-Temporal Model for Human Mobility Forecasting

2025

Authors: Romain Haton, Hocine Guia, Boualem Mokeddem, Vincent Gauthier, Amel Bouzeghoub

Published in: The 24th IEEE/WIC International Conference on Web Intelligence and Intelligent Agent Technology (WI-IAT), 2025.

Abstract

Predicting human mobility is an increasingly important field of research for applications such as urban planning, mobile healthcare services, and pandemic modeling. Several methods exist for mobility prediction. They mainly exploit recurrences using Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) to perform next-location prediction, which has shown outstanding performance for highly predictable interaction forecasting. Still, they are often limited by their inability to handle poorly predictable events effectively, resulting in insufficient performance due to the absence of contextual information. This work proposes a new approach that integrates RNNs to capture event recurrences and Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) to add contextual information from a Knowledge Graph (KG) into the training process. This combination enables a more accurate characterization of individuals and locations, thereby improving the accuracy of the next location prediction. This method outperforms state-of-the-art methods on three datasets.
